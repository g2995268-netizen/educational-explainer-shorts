# Outlines

## Targets
- Bold, consistent dark outlines on characters and key objects
- Near-black (#0a0a0a – #1a1a1a) or dark plum
- Weight: readable at 1080×1920 (scale with resolution)
- Diagrams and text may use lighter or colored outlines when needed for contrast

## Rules
- Almost everything important has an outline.
- Outline weight stays consistent within a shot.
- Do not outline the entire background.
- Character outlines are usually darker than object outlines.
