# Grade & Color

## Targets
- High saturation (avoid washed-out looks)
- Limited palette per scene (3–5 dominant colors)
- Strong contrast between subject and background
- Text: white or black with opposing outline for readability
- No film grain by default

## Mood palettes
- Danger / tension: red + purple + black
- Science / body: cyan, green, yellow, blue
- Cosmic: deep blue + purple + white
- Neutral / interface: dark blue + neon accents

## Rules
- One clear color temperature per shot.
- Avoid muddy midtones.
- Accent colors should pop against the background.
