# Characters

## Targets
- Head-to-body ratio: ~3.5–4 head heights for adults
- Eyes: large, 0.22–0.30 of head width apart, high contrast
- Outline: bold dark (near-black or dark plum), consistent weight
- Expression: readable at small size (eyes + simple mouth/brows)
- Hands: simple, ink-dot or minimal fingers when needed
- Contact shadow: soft, under feet, ~0.4–0.6 opacity

## Rules
- Characters can be humans, viruses, organs, or objects with faces.
- Avoid excessive detail. Clarity > realism.
- Blink and small idle motion preferred over static holds.
- When a character reacts, the reaction lands 0–3 frames before the spoken word.

## Common issues to avoid
- Eyes too small
- Outline too thin or inconsistent
- Static character for >12 frames without any motion
- Unreadable expression at thumbnail size
